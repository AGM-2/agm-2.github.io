AGM² is a notice board for people who work on analytical and geometric methods
for microstructure: news, papers, events and job openings, in one place and in
a monthly email.

Microstructure here means how the constituents of a continuum (molecules,
grains, crystals, cells, fibres) are organised below the scale at which it is
described as a continuum. This organisation often comes from their collective
behaviour, such as alignment or ordering. It enters the continuum description
as extra fields, such as a director, a Q-tensor or an orientation
distribution, and it shapes how the material behaves at large scales. The
topics include:

- **ordered fluids**: nematic and smectic liquid crystals, Q-tensors and their
  defects, ferrofluids, active matter, kinetic and continuum models;
- **ice and geophysics**: crystal fabric of ice, anisotropic flow of glaciers
  and ice sheets;
- **solids and elasticity**: liquid crystal elastomers and networks, origami,
  prestrained shells, shape-morphing structures;
- **microstructure in solids**: twins and laminates, shape-memory alloys,
  martensitic phase transitions;
- **biological matter**: nematic order in cell layers and tissues, collagen and
  other fibrous tissues, membranes, bacterial colonies;
- **analysis and geometry**: calculus of variations, constrained problems and
  variational inequalities, homogenisation and Γ-convergence, defects and
  topology, geometry of order-parameter spaces;
- **numerical methods**: finite element, finite volume, spectral and particle
  methods, Monte Carlo simulation, solvers and preconditioners,
  structure-preserving discretisations.
